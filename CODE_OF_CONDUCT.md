# Code of Conduct

We want Living Interfaces to be a useful, welcoming project.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback,
and make space for contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and
deliberate disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
